# Behavioral: Tell me about a time you showed leadership or influenced without authority

**Common phrasings:**
- "Tell me about a time you led something without a formal leadership role."
- "Describe a time you influenced a decision or drove change."
- "Tell me about a time you mentored someone or helped the team grow."

---

## STAR Answer (Primary — Threat Model V-Team)

**Situation:**
Microsoft's MCI (Cloud for Industries) organization had a cross-team v-team focused on threat modeling — reviewing the security architecture of products across the entire org. This was voluntary, required formal certification, and was separate from my regular engineering work.

**Task:**
Get certified as a threat modeler and contribute by reviewing architectures for other teams — providing security expertise beyond my own team's scope.

**Action:**
- Completed the Threat Modeling course and passed the tabletop exam to get certified.
- Proactively applied the skills in my own team's work — security decisions I made (VNet, managed identity, private endpoints) were informed by threat modeling principles.
- Conducted a formal **threat model architecture review for the Industry AI team** (DTT/DMF — Data Movement Framework) — identifying potential threats in their product design.
- Brought this expertise into customer engagements, adding security considerations to POC discussions.

**Result:**
- Established as a security resource for the broader MCI org, not just my team.
- Manager: *"Devesh was also certified and part of Threat Model v-team in MCI and always brought that experience while working with customer engagements, building features."*

## Alternate Example — Frontend Leadership in Power Automate (2025)

**Situation:** After ramping up on React/Fluent UI as a newcomer to the Power Automate team, I became one of the more experienced frontend contributors. New engineers were joining the team with questions about FE patterns and implementation approaches.

**Action:** Proactively unblocked teammates on FE design questions, reviewed their code, and helped guide implementation decisions — even though I had no formal tech lead title.

**Result:** Manager: *"The way he progressed in supporting other developers in FE design and work, unblocking them and guiding them towards completion is highly commendable."* Also played a key role in estimating future G2PC UI work based on my accumulated FE expertise.
