# Behavioral: Tell me about a time you collaborated effectively with others

**Common phrasings:**
- "Tell me about a time you worked cross-functionally."
- "Describe a situation where collaboration was key to success."
- "Tell me about a time you worked with people outside your immediate team."

---

## STAR Answer (Primary — Rolls Royce HMI 2025 Demo)

**Situation:**
The HMI 2025 trade show was a major customer-facing event where our team needed to demonstrate the new Copilot Assistant capability on real Rolls Royce manufacturing data. This required tight coordination across engineering, PM, and the customer's own team — across different time zones and with a hard deadline.

**Task:**
Collaborate with the Rolls Royce team and our PM to deliver an end-to-end demo that answered their specific P0 questions using the assistant, with a polished and reliable demo flow.

**Action:**
- Worked directly with the Rolls Royce team to assist with data ingestion of their proprietary dataset into our platform.
- Analyzed their P0 questions and built custom ADX functions specifically tailored to their data model.
- Partnered with the PM team to design the demo narrative — ensuring the assistant's responses mapped cleanly to what the audience would find compelling.
- Iterated on the assistant configuration (instructions, examples, views) based on feedback from the Rolls Royce side until all P0 questions were answered correctly.
- Conducted end-to-end rehearsals to ensure the demo was reliable under trade show conditions.

**Result:**
- Demo ran successfully at HMI 2025 — received strong reception from the manufacturing industry audience.
- Manager: *"View creation instead of creating complex traversal logic was key in avoiding hallucinations. This is one of the most sought after and advertised capability from the product and at big manufacturing events like HMI."*

## Alternate Example — AKS Deployment (Sep 2022)

**Situation:** Needed to containerize and deploy two services (IDS service and worker service) to AKS — first time working with Docker and Kubernetes.

**Action:** Paired with Suresh (infrastructure expert) to write Dockerfiles, configure YML pipeline files, and test the images in the AKS cluster. Divided the work clearly — I handled the Dockerfiles and code changes, Suresh handled the deployment pipeline.

**Result:** Successfully deployed both services to AKS for Dogfood. Manager: *"Though it was first time for him w.r.t CI/CD, he was able to quickly grasp the context."*
